## Course Description
The course gives a wide introduction to applied econometrics. It introduces the student to methodologies for analyzing economic problems using quantitative methods. There will be an emphasis on linking microeconomic theory to estimation techniques, and interpreting the results of various quantitative exercises using the R software environment for statistical computing and graphics. Upon completing this course, students should have become proficient in developing and interpreting linear regression models as applied to various economic problems and datasets.

## Curriculum

Textbook: [Principles of Econometrics](https://principlesofeconometrics.com/poe5/poe5.html){:target="blank"} 5th Edition (POE5), Wiley - 2018. Available at the campus bookstore Akademika.

By R. Carter Hill, William E. Griffiths, and Guay C. Lim. POE5 is an introductory book for students in economics and finance, as well as students in accounting, agricultural economics, marketing, public policy, sociology, law, and political science. It assumes that students have a formal background with courses in the principles of economics/microeconomics, and elementary mathematics and statistics.

Curriculum: The first eight chapters of the book and the appendices.

### Part 1 — Closed book (70% of the final grade)

You will use the WISEflow lockdown browser. You cannot open your own files or notes, use R or other applications, or access the internet.

In WISEflow, you will have access to a **calculator** and a **formula sheet** in both English and Norwegian. Before the exam, review the formula sheet and practise choosing and applying the relevant formulas.

- [Formula sheet in English](https://github.com/uit-sok-3081-h26/uit-sok-3081-h26.github.io/blob/main/lectures/SOK-3081_formula_sheet.pdf)
- [Formula sheet in Norwegian](https://github.com/uit-sok-3081-h26/uit-sok-3081-h26.github.io/blob/main/lectures/SOK-3081_formelsamling.pdf)

You can expect true/false, multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blank and drag-and-drop questions, as well as questions that require a numerical answer.

Enter your answers directly in WISEflow. **You must submit Part 1 before starting Part 2. Once you submit Part 1, you cannot return to it.** Follow the invigilators’ instructions when moving to Part 2.

### Part 2 — Open book (30% of the final grade)

You will analyse a dataset from the course textbook in R, estimate econometric models and interpret your results. Enter your written answers directly in the WISEflow text boxes.

You may use R, files saved on your laptop, digital notes and textbooks, course materials and R code from Canvas, and internet resources, including AI under the rules below. **This is an individual exam. During the exam, you may communicate only with the invigilators.** Cite external sources you use in your answers.

#### What to include in R-based answers

State your answer clearly and interpret the result when asked. Include the following in the relevant WISEflow text box.

- The R code you used.
- The relevant numerical output from running the code.
- A brief explanation of how the code answers the question.

Paste the code and output into WISEflow as text. A correct procedure can earn partial credit even if your final answer is incorrect.

#### Using AI in Part 2

You may use AI in Part 2 under **UiT’s Level 4 rules**. For example, you may use it to write or debug R code. You are responsible for checking the code, results and explanations you submit. Use tools permitted under UiT’s guidelines, such as ChatUiT.

If you use AI, state in the designated text box **which tools you used, what tasks you asked each tool to do, and how you checked its output**. Identify AI-generated material where you use it in your answer, whether you quote or paraphrase it. Keep relevant chat excerpts in case UiT asks to see them. You do not need to submit your chat logs with the exam.

[UiT’s exam rules and guidance on AI use (Norwegian)](https://uit.no/eksamen)
